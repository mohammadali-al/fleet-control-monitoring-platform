Create monitor job

Enter the URL first: the origin and path are derived from it.

Optional but shown first: it is the more important field. Derived: origin https://shop.example.test, path /checkout. Two subdomains or two ports are different origins.
No duplicate job may exist for the same (ip, origin).
Several paths of one origin are allowed.

Probes are chosen for you: ICMP to the IP, and with a URL also a TCP connect to its port and an HTTP(S) probe per path. The targets are spread over the monitor agents (2-3 each).
