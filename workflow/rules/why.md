# Why (one line per rule)

- R-01 — the Touches list is the single best guard against drift and two doers colliding.
- R-02 — invisible bidi characters slipped into briefs in the prior project and caused silent bugs.
- R-03 — a hand-edited board grew to ~950 lines; generating it keeps it to a screen.
- R-04 — state in the task + supervisor-only notes keep history trustworthy.
- R-05 — accidental deletes are expensive and hard to notice in review.
- R-06 — real data loss/leak is the one irreversible failure; doers never touch it.
- R-07 — throwaway dirs and stray processes were cleaned by hand before; the footer makes it machine-run.
- R-08 — briefs that contradicted the code stopped doers after their start-up cost was paid.
